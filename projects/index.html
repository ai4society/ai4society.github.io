Neuro-Symbolic and Automated Planning

Generalized Planning

Neuro-Symbolic AI Automated Planning

A suite of projects aimed at building foundation models and agentic frameworks that can reason, plan, and adapt across diverse domains. This line of work includes PlanFM, our effort to build compact foundation models for planning-like tasks through state-centric representations, tokenization, and learned transition models. Our work also spans the development of transformer-based architectures for learning sequential decision-making tasks, the design of agentic frameworks inspired by Thinking, Fast and Slow theories, and the creation of ontologies to support structured reasoning and planning.

Team: University of South Carolina, IBM Research, University of Udine, University of Brescia

This project develops resource-efficient Multi-Agent Path Finding methods for robotic deployments. The HI-MAPF framework combines decentralized planning with lightweight conflict-triggered coordination to reduce inter-agent information sharing, and related maPO work provides ontology-driven explanations for planner behavior.

Trustworthy Group Recommendation

Recommender Systems Trusted AI

We study the problem of trustworthy group recommendation, where the focus goes beyond individual user preferences and dives into collective tastes and interests. Our objective is to build novel methods and useful tools for group recommendation with fairness, and drive different use cases. This work also connects to our Meal Recommendation and Exercise Recommendation pages for wellness-related applications. We extend this problem to the team formation domain, where we present an AI-based system to aid in collaborative teaming for researchers responding to funding agency proposals. Our approach leverages NLP techniques to extract and normalize technical skills from various data sources, facilitating matching and teaming based on constraints. We have gathered initial feedback from university researchers to deploy the prototype system and published a dataset for broader use.

Team: Siva Likitha Valluru, Sai Teja Paladi, Tarmo Koppel, Biplav Srivastava

Rubik's Cube

Automated Planning

Rubik's Cube (RC) is a popular puzzle that is also computationally hard to solve. In this demonstration, we introduce the first PDDL formulation for the 3-dimension RC and solve it with off-the-shelf Fast-Downward planner. We also create a plan executor and visualizer to show how the plan achieves the intended goal. Our system has two audiences:(a) planning researchers who can explore a hard problem, and (b) RC learners wanting to learn how to solve the puzzle at their own pace.

Team: Bharath Muppasani, Vishal Pallagani, Kausik Lakkaraju, Biplav Srivastava, Forest Agostinelli
